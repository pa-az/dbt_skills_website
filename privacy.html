Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Welcome to DBT Skills Guide. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the integrity of your personal information. This Privacy Policy details our approach to data security, showing what we collect, what we store, and how we handle it.

Summary: The DBT Skills Guide application collects zero personal data, maintains zero user accounts, and stores all user logs exclusively on your local device.

1. Collection of Personal Information

We do not collect any personal identifier information. When you use the DBT Skills Guide app:

  • You are not required to create an account or provide registration credentials.
  • We do not collect or request names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • We do not gather biological, medical, or clinical information from your device.

2. Storage of App Data & Worksheets

Any data you enter in the app, including distress scale ratings, journal reflections, checkmarks, and worksheets (such as STOP, ACCEPTS, and Self-Soothing tools) is stored strictly on your local device.

This local data is saved securely using standard storage utilities. We do not use cloud databases or remote servers to backup or transfer your records. If you delete the app from your device, your local journal history and records will be deleted permanently.

3. Web Application Usage

For users running the web version of the application:

  • Your worksheet entries are saved locally in your browser's secure cache (`localStorage`).
  • This information is never transmitted to our web servers or hosting providers.
  • Clearing your browser's data/cache will erase your stored worksheets.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

We value a distraction-free, private environment for mindfulness:

  • We do not integrate any third-party tracking SDKs, advertising APIs, or tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell, trade, or share user data because we collect none.

5. Notifications

The app can send you an optional daily practice reminder:

  • Reminders are off until you turn them on in Settings, and you choose the time.
  • They are scheduled locally by your device. There is no push server, and no notification is sent over the internet.
  • The reminder time is stored on your device alongside your other settings. It is never transmitted.
  • You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings, or revoke the notification permission in your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

DBT Skills Guide is intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because the app collects no personal data at all and transmits nothing off your device.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date.

8. Support Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please email us at mycreatz@outlook.com.
